# Task 4.3 — one-painter and one-position-channel evidence

## Scope

This record covers the migrated Emby Browser destinations: hero-bearing Movies,
Emby homevideos/feed-group, Emby podcast-channel browsing, and narrow TV Series
browsing, plus the Wide TV right rail. It records reachable call chains and
named carve-outs only; it does not use raw symbol-absence claims. Inline Search,
legacy non-hero two-column lists, and Music/album rendering remain outside the
canonical Browser hero path.

## 1. Canonical Wide one-painter chain

The canonical Browser Wide decision is the `BrowserComponent::view` `wide`
guard at `src/app/components/browser/mod.rs:538-541`. When that guard is true,
the view arm at `src/app/components/browser/mod.rs:592-594` calls
`BrowserComponent::render_wide_movies`. That painter is defined at
`src/app/components/browser/paint.rs:23-30` and its ordinary list path at
`src/app/components/browser/paint.rs:143-153` calls the shared
`render_wide_media_list` painter at `src/app/render/components/media_list.rs:223-227`.
The defensive degenerate-rect branch in `paint.rs:35-49` also calls that same
canonical painter, not a second list painter.

The same-rect predicate proof is:

- `BrowserComponent::view` evaluates `shared_hero_presentation(area)` at
  `src/app/components/browser/mod.rs:538-541`.
- `render_wide_movies` assigns `let body_area = area` at
  `src/app/components/browser/paint.rs:31`, then calls
  `wide_library_panes(body_area, ...)` at `paint.rs:35-36`.
- `wide_library_panes` forwards that exact `area` unchanged to
  `hero_left::shared_hero_presentation(area)` at
  `src/app/render/arrangements/library.rs:12-18`.
- The pure predicate itself is the single width/height expression at
  `src/app/render/arrangements/hero_left.rs:37-40`.

Thus the Wide gate and the painter's pane admission test are the same pure
predicate on the same `Rect`; an accepted Browser Wide frame follows the
canonical hero + `render_wide_media_list` chain.

The Inline Search carve-out is a separate reachable chain:
`InlineSearchComponent::view` builds its own `LibraryListRenderCtx` and calls
`render_generic_movies_home_video_rows_with_ctx` at
`src/app/components/inline_search.rs:200-228`; that legacy function is defined
at `src/app/render/components/list.rs:6-40` and selects its grouped/plain legacy
row painter there. It is not reached through the Browser Wide guard above, so
this explicitly non-applicable Inline Search path does not create a second
canonical Wide painter.

## 2. Persistent Narrow control

`BrowserComponent` stores `inline_browser: InlineMediaBrowser<usize>` at
`src/app/components/browser/mod.rs:84-86` and constructs it once during
component initialization at `src/app/components/browser/mod.rs:98-114` (the
construction is at line 113). Browser's non-Wide view passes that same control
by mutable reference to `render_narrow_browse_with_ctx` at
`src/app/components/browser/mod.rs:597-610`.

The narrow painter accepts `browser: &mut InlineMediaBrowser<usize>` in its
signature at `src/app/render/components/list_narrow.rs:30-38`. For a
hero-bearing narrow surface, its `hero_presentation` branch at
`list_narrow.rs:146-151` calls `render_inline_media_browser`, and the result's
painted geometry is consumed at `list_narrow.rs:160-174`. This is the persistent
control-owned Narrow path; it does not construct a per-frame control.

## 3. Canonical geometry and replacement-plan carve-outs

The canonical Narrow hero branch takes its geometry from the painter result:
`list_narrow.rs:150-174` copies `layout.left_row_map` from
`result.row_geometry`, and also derives `selected_item_rect` from that same
`row_geometry` at lines 172-174. The selected-row replacement is therefore
budgeted and painted once by the active `InlineMediaBrowser`.

The remaining `InlineReplacementPlan::new` sites have these reachable owners:

- `src/app/render/components/media_list.rs:63-70` is inside
  `render_plain_rows` (`media_list.rs:27-30`). Its callers at
  `src/app/render/components/list_narrow.rs:178-184` are the explicit
  non-hero branch (`list_narrow.rs:146-185`), while the legacy generic/Inline
  Search route reaches it through `src/app/render/components/list.rs:31-40`.
  The same shared plain-row site is also used by the Music wide group at
  `src/app/render/components/music_wide.rs:425-426`. These are the legacy
  non-hero/two-column and Music carve-outs, not the canonical Browser hero
  branch.
- `src/app/render/components/list_letter_groups.rs:98-105` is reached by the
  grouped legacy branch at `src/app/render/components/list.rs:27-31` and by the
  explicit non-hero branch at `src/app/render/components/list_narrow.rs:174-178`.
  Its own documentation identifies this as the non-music legacy grouped-list
  policy (`list_letter_groups.rs:17-18`).
- `src/app/render/components/album_inline.rs:52-59` is the Music album
  replacement plan. `src/app/render/components/album.rs:228` is its caller,
  and `album_inline.rs:18-22` names the grouped album-inline renderer.

No canonical Wide or Narrow hero chain above traverses these legacy plan
sites; the canonical Narrow chain consumes `InlinePaintResult.row_geometry`
instead.

## 4. Browser's single control-owned position channel

`BrowserContent` is position-free: its fields are listed at
`src/app/components/browser/content.rs:22-32`, with no cursor/scroll fields.
The sole reconstitution site inside the Browser component directory is
`BrowserContent::with_cursor_scroll` at
`src/app/components/browser/content.rs:80-95`; it calls
`LibraryListRenderCtx::from_items` at line 85 using the `cursor` and `scroll`
arguments supplied by the Browser's control-owned state. Browser's
`self.context` and feed projection invoke that helper at
`src/app/components/browser/mod.rs:572-586`, passing `self.cursor` and
`self.scroll`.

The shell content seam strips position before the component boundary:
`push_emby_browser_content` constructs the feed-group render context at
`src/app/shell_browser.rs:308-315` or the ordinary context through
`library_list_render_ctx` at `shell_browser.rs:319-327`, then calls
`BrowserContent::from_render_ctx` at `shell_browser.rs:342`. The component
receives that position-free value via `browser.set_content(content, focused)`
at `shell_browser.rs:347`. The explicit identity gate at
`shell_browser.rs:347-353` calls `browser.apply_position(cursor, scroll)` only
when `note_browse_identity` returns true. The identity fields and gate are
implemented at `src/app/components/browser/mod.rs:155-171`; ordinary pushes
therefore retain control-owned cursor/scroll rather than re-seeding them.

The shell's `render_emby_browser_component` reads the control's cursor/scroll
for its App-owned poster-prefetch and persistence seams at
`src/app/shell_browser.rs:430-452`; this is a consumer of control state, not a
second paint-position channel.
